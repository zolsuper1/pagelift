          Questions

          Why not just use ChatGPT?

          You can. For one product, with time to spare, it works. Here's what you'd be doing by hand each time: copying the product details in, checking that nothing got invented, trimming the title to fit Google, writing alt text for every image, and pasting each field back. For 20 or 100 products, add keeping the tone consistent and building an import file. Pagelift reads your page itself, writes only from your own details, shows every field's character count against its limit, and hands you a CSV ready for Shopify. And if you don't like it, you get your money back.

          Will it sound like AI?

          It's written in plain American English, without empty phrases like "perfect" or "premium quality". It never invents sizes, materials or prices: it only writes from what's on your page. When shoppers would look for something that's missing, you get a list of what to add.

          What about SEO?

          Titles stay under 60 characters and lead with the main search term. You also get a meta description under 155 characters and alt text for every image. Those are the fields Google reads. No ranking promises, but the basics will be right.

          How do I get it into my store?

          Shopify: download the CSV from your result page, go to Products, then Import, and choose to overwrite products with matching handles. WooCommerce, or any single product: copy each field with one click and paste it into the product editor.
